# Air Ghost

**Hook:** A little ghost that blushes red when your room needs fresh air.

**Description (60 words):**
Air Ghost measures real CO₂ with a Sensirion photoacoustic sensor, not a guess, and blushes the colour
of your air: green is fresh, orange is getting stuffy, red means open a window. Boop its "O" mouth for
temperature and humidity colours. It runs from any USB-C charger and keeps meetings, homework and naps
awake. Spooky? Only the stale air is.
